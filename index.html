Rules for Choosing the Perfect Rug

Your living room is an important space. Keep these tips in mind when choosing a rug:

  • Ensure the rug is the right size for the space.
  • Colour and pattern should complement your decor.
  • Match the style, traditional or modern, to the room.
  • Lighting affects how the rug looks.
  • Consider the material of the rug.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between a rug and a carpet?

A carpet is typically wall-to-wall flooring or a large roll covering a room. A rug is a finished piece that covers a specific area and anchors the decor.

Do you deliver?

Yes, we can arrange delivery. Message us on WhatsApp with your location for details.

How do I choose the right rug size?

Make sure at least the front legs of your sofa and chairs rest on the rug so the seating area feels connected.

Do you supply masjid carpets?

Yes. We supply high-density saf carpets and prayer rugs for mosques and community spaces.

How do I clean my rug?

Vacuum regularly without a rotating brush, blot spills with a dry cloth (never scrub), and use professional washing for deep cleaning.
